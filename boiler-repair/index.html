Why Boilers Are Rare Here, But Not Nonexistent

Most homes built in Cary and the surrounding towns over the past few decades use heat pumps or forced-air gas furnaces rather than a boiler and radiator or radiant floor system. Boilers are more common in colder climates with a longer history of that heating style.

That said, a real minority of homes here, usually older properties, custom builds, or homes relocated from a different climate's design conventions, do have a boiler system, and it needs the same kind of service as anywhere else when something goes wrong.

Being honest about how uncommon boilers are here matters for one practical reason: parts availability. A mainstream water heater part is often on a truck same-day, while a specific boiler component sometimes needs to be special ordered, which can affect repair timelines more than the actual repair difficulty does.

Signs To Watch For

Signs Your Boiler Needs Service

No Heat Output

A boiler that's running but not producing heat often points to a pump or valve issue.

Leaking At The Unit

Water pooling near the boiler usually means a failing seal or pressure relief valve.

Unusual Noise

Banging or kettling sounds often indicate mineral buildup or trapped air in the system.

Pressure Dropping Repeatedly

A boiler that keeps losing pressure has a leak somewhere in the closed loop, even if it isn't visible.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you actually service boilers, given how rare they are here?

Yes, though it's a smaller share of our calls than water heater service. We service what's out there.

Is my old boiler worth repairing or should I switch systems?

That depends on the boiler's age, the cost of the specific repair, and whether parts are still readily available. We give you a straight comparison.

Can you source parts for an older or unusual boiler brand?

In most cases yes, though it can take longer than a mainstream water heater part, which we'll tell you upfront.

Is switching from a boiler to a heat pump system a plumbing job?

That's a larger HVAC conversion project beyond standard plumbing scope. We can advise on the plumbing-specific portion of that conversation.

How often should a boiler be serviced?

Annual maintenance is generally recommended, similar to an HVAC system, to catch small issues before they become larger repairs.

Is a leaking boiler an emergency?

Active leaking warrants a prompt call, though it's rarely as urgent as a burst water pipe. Shutting down the system if safe to do so limits further loss in the meantime.

Do you install new boiler systems, or only repair existing ones?

We primarily service existing systems. A new boiler installation is a larger conversation we can discuss based on the specific home and system involved.

Are replacement parts for older boilers hard to find?

It varies by brand and age. We confirm parts availability upfront so there are no surprises on repair timeline.

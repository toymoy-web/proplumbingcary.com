Why Some Clogs Keep Coming Back

A drain that clears with snaking and then clogs again within a few weeks usually isn't a soap-and-hair problem anymore. Root intrusion in an older sewer line or a partial pipe collapse both produce exactly that pattern, and neither one is fixed by snaking the same spot again.

In Cary, this shows up most in the established neighborhoods with mature trees, where older clay or cast iron sewer laterals in areas like Lochmere are more prone to root intrusion than the PVC lines typical of newer Amberly or Copperleaf construction.

That distinction matters for the fix, not just the diagnosis. Snaking again buys a few weeks at most against a root problem, while hydro jetting actually clears the roots from the pipe wall, and a camera inspection tells us whether the line itself needs a trenchless repair rather than another round of clearing.

Grease buildup in a kitchen line behaves differently again, narrowing the pipe gradually rather than blocking it all at once, which is why a kitchen drain that's been slow for months often needs jetting rather than a quick snake to actually clear the buildup coating the pipe wall.

Signs To Watch For

Signs A Clog Needs More Than Snaking

Repeat Clogs

The same drain clogging again within weeks points to a root or structural issue, not debris.

Multiple Fixtures Backing Up

If more than one drain backs up at once, the problem is likely in the main line, not one fixture.

Gurgling Drains

Gurgling from a drain that isn't currently being used usually means air is trapped by a venting problem or a main-line blockage further down the system.

Slow Everywhere

Every drain in the house running slow points to the main line, not individual traps.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you tell what's causing a repeat clog?

Yes, camera inspection shows exactly what's in the line, whether that's roots, grease, or a pipe issue.

Do you clear main sewer line clogs, not just sink drains?

Yes. See our leak detection page if the issue turns out to be a cracked line rather than a simple clog.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipe?

In most cases yes, and we confirm pipe condition first since it's more effective than snaking for grease and root buildup.

How do I know if I need a camera inspection or just a snake?

If this is the first time the drain has clogged, snaking is usually enough. If it's clogged before, a camera inspection is worth doing before paying for another round of snaking that likely won't hold.

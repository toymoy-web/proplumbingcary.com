Why Disposals Jam Or Stop Working

A disposal that hums but doesn't spin usually has a jammed impeller, often from something hard or fibrous caught inside. A unit that doesn't respond at all is more likely a tripped reset button or an electrical issue rather than a mechanical jam.

Leaks usually show up at one of three points: the sink flange seal, the discharge pipe connection, or the dishwasher inlet, and each one is a different fix even though the symptom looks the same from under the sink.

Because a jam, an electrical fault, and a leak all present differently under close inspection even though they can look similar at first glance, diagnosing before quoting avoids paying for a replacement unit when a cleared jam or a reset would have solved it.

Signs To Watch For

Signs Of A Disposal Problem

Humming, Not Spinning

A humming sound with no spinning usually means a jammed impeller, often fixable without replacement.

No Response At All

Complete silence often points to a tripped reset button or an electrical supply issue.

Leaking Underneath

Water pooling under the unit traces to the flange seal, discharge pipe, or dishwasher connection.

Unusual Grinding Noise

Grinding beyond normal operation often means something hard has damaged the impeller blades.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is it safe to try clearing a jam myself?

Never with your hand. A disposal wrench or the reset button handles most jams safely, but if that doesn't work, it's worth having it looked at rather than forcing it.

How long do garbage disposals typically last?

Around 8 to 12 years under normal household use, though heavy use or hard items going down it shortens that.

Can you install a disposal where there wasn't one before?

Yes, including the electrical and drain connections needed for a first-time installation.

Could this actually be a faucet issue instead?

See our faucet & sink repair page if the leak is coming from above the disposal rather than the unit itself.

What size disposal do I actually need?

Household size and how heavily the kitchen is used both factor in. We recommend a size based on your actual use, not just the biggest available unit.

Can a disposal damage my dishwasher if it's clogged?

A clogged disposal can back water up into the dishwasher through the shared connection, which is one more reason to address a clog promptly.

Why does my disposal smell bad even when it's working fine?

Trapped food particles in the splash guard or grinding chamber cause most odors, and a routine cleaning often resolves it without needing a repair.

Is a louder-than-usual hum normal for an older unit?

Some added noise is normal with age, but a sudden change in sound usually signals a developing issue worth having checked.

Can you install a disposal with a specific brand I prefer?

Yes, we install the brand and horsepower you prefer, or can recommend one based on your household's typical use.

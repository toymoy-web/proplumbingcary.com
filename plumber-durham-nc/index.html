We Cover South Durham, Not The Whole City

Durham is a large city with its own long-established local plumbers, especially downtown and in North Durham. Rather than claim coverage we can't actually back up with the same response time we offer Cary, we scope our Durham coverage to the South Durham and Research Triangle Park side of the city, the part closest to our own base and the part that sits on our existing daily routes.

That area includes neighborhoods like Woodcroft, Hope Valley Farms, and Davis Park, all built out over the last few decades as Research Triangle Park brought jobs and growth to this side of Durham. It's a newer story than the city's downtown core, which traces back to Durham's 1869 incorporation and its tobacco-industry history.

South Durham is about 19 miles and a 25-minute drive from Cary via I-40, with NC-55 offering a direct second route through the Research Triangle Park corridor itself. Emergency Prep

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

1

Shut Off The Main Valve

If it's safe to reach, shutting off the main water valve stops the source before anything else.

2

Kill Power To Wet Areas

If water is near outlets or a panel and it's reachable without stepping into water, shut off power to that area.

3

Clear The Immediate Area

Move what you can safely reach out of the way of spreading water before help arrives.

4

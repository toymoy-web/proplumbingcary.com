Historic Roots

Fuquay-Varina's Two Historic Districts

Fuquay-Varina was formed in 1963 when the two original towns of Fuquay Springs and Varina merged, though the area was incorporated as far back as 1909. the twin Fuquay Springs and Varina Commercial Historic Districts downtown still stand, with Victorian, Craftsman, and Colonial Revival homes dating back to the late 1800s and early 1900s alongside the Fuquay Springs and Varina Commercial Historic Districts, home to the Ben-Wiley Hotel and Fuquay Mineral Spring Park.

That history matters for plumbing more than it might seem. Homes in and around the historic districts run some of the oldest supply lines in the network's entire coverage area, calling most often for leak detection, while the newer subdivisions ringing the town, including Bridgeton and Bella Casa, see a completely different set of calls tied to recent construction instead.

Fuquay-Varina is south of Cary and Holly Springs, roughly 35 minutes from Raleigh, which puts it at the edge of our regular coverage rather than the center of it, though it's still on the same daily schedule as closer towns.

The 1963 merger of Fuquay Springs and Varina into a single town is part of why the historic downtown reads almost like two adjoining districts rather than one continuous core, each with its own architectural character and, in practical terms, its own mix of original pipe materials that a technician has to account for separately.

Emergency Prep

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

1

Shut Off The Main Valve

If it's safe to reach, shutting off the main water valve stops the source before anything else.

2

Kill Power To Wet Areas

If water is near outlets or a panel and it's reachable without stepping into water, shut off power to that area.

3

Clear The Immediate Area

Move what you can safely reach out of the way of spreading water before help arrives.

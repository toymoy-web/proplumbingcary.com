Local Context

Garner's History And What It Means Today

Growth Along US 70

Garner was incorporated in 1905, growing up along US 70, which became the first paved highway in North Carolina in 1917, and that early highway access shaped much of its older residential core.

Edenwood

An established Garner neighborhood with a service pattern similar to the historic downtown.

Aversboro & Vandora Springs

Newer and mixed-era neighborhoods seeing a wider variety of calls.

Local Utility

Garner runs the Town of Garner's own water utility, worth knowing if a water-quality question comes up alongside a plumbing one.

24/7 Dispatch

Garner is just south of Raleigh and about 7 miles southeast of Cary, which places it on the same regular coverage as our other southern and eastern Wake County stops. All work here follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted by the North Carolina State Building Code.

Garner's growth followed US 70 outward from the original Main Street core, which is why the town's housing stock reads almost like a timeline moving away from downtown: the oldest homes cluster near the historic district, with each successive ring of development a bit newer than the last as the town expanded along the highway corridor.

Emergency Prep

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

1

Shut Off The Main Valve

If it's safe to reach, shutting off the main water valve stops the source before anything else.

2

Kill Power To Wet Areas

If water is near outlets or a panel and it's reachable without stepping into water, shut off power to that area.

3

Clear The Immediate Area

Move what you can safely reach out of the way of spreading water before help arrives.

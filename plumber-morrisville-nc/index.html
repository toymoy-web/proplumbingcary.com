Housing Timeline

Morrisville's Housing Timeline

Morrisville was founded in 1852, now home to Lenovo's North American operational headquarters, but the town most people know today grew up around the Research Triangle's tech boom. That split, older streets near downtown against a large share of 2000s-and-newer construction, shows up directly in the plumbing calls we get.

The older church street area near downtown still runs original supply lines old enough for leak detection and water heater calls, while the newer construction that makes up most of the town, including Weston and Davis Park, sees more first-time fixture and installation work instead.

Morrisville is directly north of Cary, positioned between Raleigh and Durham in the Research Triangle, which puts it on the same daily route structure as Cary and Apex rather than requiring a separate dispatch schedule. That regional position, sitting between two larger cities, is also part of why the town has grown as fast as it has, which in turn is why so much of its housing stock skews newer than a typical Wake County town its age would suggest.

1

Shut Off The Main Valve

2

Kill Power To Wet Areas

If water is near outlets or a panel and it's reachable without stepping into water, shut off power to that area.

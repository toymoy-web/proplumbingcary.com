What A Plumbing Installation Actually Involves

Adding a new bathroom, moving a kitchen sink, or finishing a basement all require running new supply and drain lines to code, not just connecting a fixture to whatever happens to be nearby.

In Cary, this most often comes up during kitchen or bathroom remodels in established neighborhoods, where the existing plumbing layout wasn't designed for the new fixture placement a homeowner wants.

Getting the rough-in plumbing right before walls and flooring go in matters more here than in almost any other part of a remodel, since a venting or slope mistake discovered afterward means opening up finished work rather than an open stud wall.

Signs To Watch For

When You Need A Plumbing Installation Quote

Adding A New Fixture

A new bathroom, wet bar, or laundry hookup all need new supply and drain runs.

Relocating An Existing Fixture

Moving a sink or toilet even a few feet often means rerouting the drain line's slope and venting.

Finishing A Basement Or Addition

New square footage typically needs plumbing brought in from scratch, not extended from the nearest existing line.

Remodel In Progress

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you work directly with my contractor or builder?

Yes, we coordinate rough-in timing directly with a general contractor's schedule on remodels and additions.

Can you install plumbing for a basement bathroom?

Yes, including handling the venting and drainage challenges specific to below-grade installations.

Will you pull the required permits?

Permit requirements depend on scope. We can advise on what's needed for your specific project.

What if I just need one fixture replaced, not a full installation?

See our relevant service page, like toilet repair & installation or faucet & sink repair, for a straightforward swap.

Can you install a wet bar or outdoor kitchen plumbing?

Yes, including the supply and drain lines needed for a wet bar, outdoor kitchen, or similar addition.

Do you coordinate with electricians and other trades on a remodel?

Yes, we work alongside other trades on a project timeline rather than treating the plumbing scope in isolation.

How far in advance should I schedule installation for a remodel?

As early as possible once your contractor has a rough timeline, since coordinating rough-in around drywall and flooring stages matters for the schedule.

Do you handle gas line installation for a new range or grill?

Yes, new gas line installation and connection is part of our plumbing installation scope for kitchen and outdoor upgrades.

What's the difference between rough-in and finish plumbing?

Rough-in is the supply and drain lines run inside the walls before they close, and finish work is connecting the actual fixtures once the space is ready.

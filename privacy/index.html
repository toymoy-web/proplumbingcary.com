Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

When you call the number listed on this site, that call may be handled through a call-tracking service that records the call for scheduling accuracy and quality purposes, and that logs the phone number you called from. If you email us, we retain that email address and message to respond to you.

How We Use It

Purpose Of Collection

Information collected through a call or email is used to schedule service, follow up on a job, and, for recorded calls, for internal quality and training purposes. We do not sell contact information collected through this site or its phone line to third parties.

Cookies And Analytics

Website Analytics

This site may use standard analytics tools to understand general visitor traffic, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors arrived at the site. This data is aggregate and isn't used to identify individual visitors personally.

Third-Party Services

Call Tracking And Scheduling Tools

Phone numbers displayed on this site may be call-tracking numbers that route to our team through a third-party call-tracking platform. That platform's own privacy practices govern how it handles call data on its end, separate from how we use the resulting scheduling information.

Contacting Us

Questions About This Policy

If you have questions about how your information is handled, contact us at contact@proplumbingcary.com or call (919) 944-7281.

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in how the site or its tools operate. The version posted here is the one currently in effect.

Before You Call

What To Do While You Wait

Know Where The Main Shutoff Is

If a leak or burst pipe is active, shutting off the main water valve, if it's safe to reach, limits damage before help arrives.

Avoid DIY Fixes On Gas Lines

Never attempt to repair or disconnect a gas line yourself. If you smell gas, leave the area and call the gas utility before calling us.

Standards We Work To

Code Compliance

Plumbing work in Cary follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted by the North Carolina State Building Code, and North Carolina requires plumbing contractors to hold a license issued by the NC State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler Contractors.

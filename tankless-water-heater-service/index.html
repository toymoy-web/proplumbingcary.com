Why Tankless Units Need Different Service Than A Tank

A tankless unit doesn't fail the way a tank does. Instead of sediment settling at the bottom, mineral scale builds up directly on the heat exchanger, gradually restricting flow until the unit can't keep up with demand or shuts down with an error code.

Cary's water hardness accelerates this scale buildup, which is why manufacturers generally recommend an annual flush here rather than the longer intervals sometimes quoted for softer-water regions. Skipping that flush is the single most common reason a tankless unit underperforms years before it should.

The practical effect is that a unit which seems to be failing is often just overdue for maintenance rather than actually broken. Confirming that with a flush first, before quoting a repair or a full replacement, saves the cost of parts that were never the actual issue.

Signs To Watch For

Signs Your Tankless Unit Needs Service

Reduced Hot Water Output

Water that used to run hot now runs lukewarm at the same flow rate, a classic scale symptom.

Error Code Displayed

Most manufacturers display a specific code rather than failing silently, which speeds up diagnosis.

Delayed Hot Water

A longer wait than usual for hot water to reach the tap can mean a flow sensor issue.

Unit Short-Cycling

The unit turning on and off repeatedly during use usually points to a flow or ignition problem.

FAQs

Common Questions

How often should a tankless unit be flushed?

Annually is the general guideline in Cary given local water hardness, though usage volume affects that timeline too.

Can I flush it myself?

Some homeowners do with a DIY kit, but a professional flush also inspects the flow sensor and venting, which a self-service flush skips.

Is my unit worth repairing or should I replace it?

See our water heater replacement page for that comparison if the unit is older or has needed repeated service.

Do you work on all tankless brands?

Yes, including Rinnai, Navien, and Rheem tankless lines.

Does a whole-home water softener reduce how often I need to flush?

Yes, softened water significantly slows scale buildup, which can extend the interval between flushes. See our water softener page for details.

Does the warranty cover a flush and descale visit?

Most manufacturer warranties actually require proof of regular maintenance, so a documented flush can help protect your existing warranty coverage.

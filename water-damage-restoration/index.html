Why This Is Handled Separately From The Plumbing Repair

Fixing the pipe that caused a flood and drying out the structure it damaged are two different jobs with two different sets of equipment. A plumber can stop the water. Getting the water back out of flooring, drywall, and insulation before mold sets in is a separate process.

Mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours in wet drywall or carpet padding, so the drying timeline matters as much as the initial cleanup. This applies the same way across Cary regardless of neighborhood age, since any home with a burst pipe or overflow is on the same clock.

While waiting for a technician, it's safe to remove valuables from the affected area and, if reachable safely, shut off the water source causing the flooding. Avoid running a shop vac on water near any electrical outlets or panels, and avoid walking on saturated carpet more than necessary, since that pushes water deeper into the padding underneath.

Drying equipment stays in place for several days in most cases, with moisture readings checked along the way, since pulling air movers out too early is one of the most common reasons mold shows up weeks after a cleanup that looked complete on the surface.

Signs To Watch For

Signs You Need Extraction, Not Just A Mop

Standing Water

Any water that hasn't absorbed or drained on its own within a few hours needs pump and extraction equipment, not towels or a shop vac alone.

Wet Drywall Or Baseboards

Drywall that's soft or discolored at the base has already started absorbing water into the wall cavity.

Musty Smell

A musty odor showing up within a day or two of water exposure often means mold growth has already started inside a wall cavity or under flooring.

Buckling Flooring

Warped hardwood or lifting laminate means water has gotten underneath, not just on top.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is this the same call as fixing the burst pipe?

No. See our leak detection page for tracing the source, since the pipe repair and the drying-out process are handled separately.

How fast do you respond?

This line runs 24/7 specifically because the drying window after water exposure is short.

Will insurance cover this?

Sudden water damage is typically covered, though every policy is different. We document the damage to support your claim either way.

What should I document for my insurance claim?

Photos of the standing water and affected materials before cleanup starts, along with a rough timeline of when the leak or overflow began, help support the claim.

Can you dry out a finished basement?

Yes. Finished basements take longer since drywall, flooring, and framing are all involved, but the same extraction and drying process applies, just over a larger affected area.

How do you know when a space is actually fully dry?

Moisture meters check materials at multiple points, not just the surface, since a wall or subfloor can feel dry to the touch while still holding moisture deeper inside.

Can mold be prevented if extraction happens fast enough?

Fast extraction and drying significantly reduces the risk, though it depends on how long water was present before the response started.

Do you handle the odor that sometimes lingers after water damage?

Yes, proper drying and, where needed, treatment of affected materials addresses most lingering odor rather than just masking it.

Local Cause

Why Softeners Stop Working Right

A water softener that's no longer softening usually comes down to one of two things: the resin bed has fouled and needs cleaning or replacement, or the control valve has stopped regenerating on schedule, so the resin never gets recharged.

Cary's municipal water has enough hardness that most homes benefit from softening, which is why this comes up as a common first-time installation request in neighborhoods without an existing system, not just a repair call for homes that already have one.

The practical impact of skipping this shows up gradually rather than all at once: shortened water heater life, more frequent faucet and fixture repairs, and higher soap and detergent use, none of which is dramatic enough on its own to prompt a call until it's added up over a few years.

Signs To Watch For

Signs Of A Water Softener Problem

Scale Buildup Returning

Spots on dishes and fixtures coming back means the softener isn't treating water anymore.

Soap Not Lathering Well

Hard water noticeably reduces soap and shampoo lather, a quick sign softening has stopped.

Salt Bridge In The Tank

A hardened crust of salt in the brine tank prevents proper regeneration even with salt present.

No System Installed Yet

Persistent scale on fixtures and water heaters across the whole home points to a first-time installation need.

FAQs

Common Questions

How do I know if I need a repair or a new system?

A system under warranty age with one clear fault is usually worth repairing. Repeated faults on an older unit often favor replacement.

Will a softener help with scale in my water heater?

Yes, reducing incoming hardness slows the sediment buildup that shortens water heater life. See our water heater repair page if scale has already caused symptoms.

What is a salt bridge and why does it matter?

It's a hardened crust that forms in the brine tank, blocking salt from dissolving into the water needed for regeneration, which stops the whole system from softening.

Do you install whole-home systems, not just under-sink units?

Yes, our installations are whole-home systems treating water at the point it enters the house.

How long does a typical resin bed last?

Resin generally lasts 10 to 15 years under normal use before it needs replacement, though water hardness and household use both affect that timeline.

Do you service softeners installed by another company?

Yes, we service and repair existing systems regardless of who originally installed them.

Is salt-free water conditioning an option instead of a traditional softener?

Salt-free conditioners are available and avoid sodium in the water, though traditional softening is generally more effective against heavy scale buildup.

Hard water is a common contributor to premature tank failure. If so, our Water Heater Repair page covers that directly. Scale buildup in tankless units is directly tied to water hardness. That's a job for Tankless Water Heater Service instead.

Hard water scale often affects faucet performance too. In that case, take a look at our Faucet & Sink Repair page.
